Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get help with StyleSaya?

Contact us using the support email below. Please describe the issue and include your device model and iOS version when relevant.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. Select StyleSaya to view your subscription options.

What if I paid but cannot access premium features?

Check that you're using the account associated with your purchase. If the problem continues, contact support for assistance.

How do I request account deletion?

You can contact us for help with account deletion. You can also use the account deletion option in the app if it is available.
